pick one, pick several — shown as reactions in Slack, buttons in Telegram and
buttons in the window's thread panel, and the press comes back as a typed
decision. It is ordered right after *an escalation brings its evidence*, and
the endpoint, window and events plans each name the half that lands with them.

rewrite: no database ships, but what a port persists is the truth wherever an
adapter keeps it; repositories await every port call and name no file adapter;
