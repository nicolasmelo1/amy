A plan is a declaration. The plan a note becomes is asked for as a YAML
declaration between `---` fences, its title and a few lines of why, because
every plan in the repository is one now and `L4.PLAN_IS_A_LINKED_DECLARATION`
refuses prose. Before, the prompt only said to follow the plans already there,
which taught the old shape to a draft written against the new rule.
